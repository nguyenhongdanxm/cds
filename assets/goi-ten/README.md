Trộm trứng and UFO were adapted from the public Gọi Tên Vui page supplied by the school administrator:
https://goi-ten-vui-10-game.sandypeony4.chatgpt.site/

Only these two scenes and their shared character/audio helpers and required WebP assets are included. Assets are served locally. No iframe, external script, or transfer of the school's student roster to the original site is used.
The CDS entry point requires login and learning view permission. History is stored per account hash, scene and class in this browser. It is a classroom calling aid, not a server attendance record.

Audio update: Egg uses the local MP3 excerpts in audio/. CC0 sound effects and Kevin MacLeod CC BY 4.0 music are credited in the page and audio/sources.json. Music is excerpted to 30 seconds, normalized, faded and re-encoded. UFO uses an original procedural arpeggio, engine arrival, tractor beam, descending release and success cues generated with Web Audio, plus a CC0 cartoon reaction voice. Playback starts from a user action; music/effect levels are separate and the master checkbox stops all channels. Hidden tabs stop audio until the next interaction.
