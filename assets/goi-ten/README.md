Trộm trứng and UFO were adapted from the public Gọi Tên Vui page supplied by the school administrator:
https://goi-ten-vui-10-game.sandypeony4.chatgpt.site/

Only these two scenes and their shared character/audio helpers and required WebP assets are included. Assets are served locally. No iframe, external script, or transfer of the school's student roster to the original site is used.
The CDS entry point requires login and learning view permission. History is stored per account hash, scene and class in this browser. It is a classroom calling aid, not a server attendance record.

Audio update: Egg uses the local MP3 excerpts in audio/. CC0 sound effects and Kevin MacLeod CC BY 4.0 music are credited in the page and audio/sources.json. Music is excerpted to 30 seconds, normalized, faded and re-encoded. UFO uses an original procedural arpeggio, engine arrival, tractor beam, descending release and success cues generated with Web Audio, plus a CC0 cartoon reaction voice. Playback starts from a user action; music/effect levels are separate and the master checkbox stops all channels. Hidden tabs stop audio until the next interaction.

Reaction update: removed spoken text overlays above students. Seven Vietnamese exclamations are synthesized offline with Piper vi_VN-vais1000-medium (VAIS-1000 dataset, CC BY 4.0 per its model card), with varied pitch/pace and normalized levels. MP3 files, source metadata and the model card are in audio/. Reaction playback is throttled and never queues overlapping phrases; music ducks during speech. The space score now has minor-key ostinato, low percussion and rising intensity; the tractor beam has upward rings/particles and layered sweep tones. No browser voice service or external TTS request is required during play.

Shirt-catch update: hen_grip.webp is a new transparent sprite generated from the original hen reference, retaining its illustration style. Prompt: closed beak pinching a white school-shirt hem, neck extended, braced feet, friendly convincing tug, no skin bite or injury, transparent background. The renderer crops away the unattached fabric tail and connects the beak to the selected student's shirt with tension folds, then waits 1.4 seconds before confirming. Voices use slower stable synthesis without pitch-rate changes, are preloaded after interaction and can finish at the normal result. The hen's one-time chase line is “Cục ta, cục tác! Dám trộm trứng của ta à?”.
